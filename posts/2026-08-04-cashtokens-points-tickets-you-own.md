# The Loyalty Points Trap: How CashTokens Let You Actually Own What You Earn

Raise your hand if you've ever watched a pile of hard-earned loyalty points quietly rot away. Airline miles you swore you'd use. A coffee-shop card with a couple of free lattes on it. That gift card from last Christmas, still in a drawer, worth slightly less than the paper it's printed on.

These are the "ghost assets" of modern life. And here's the uncomfortable truth: **you never really owned any of them.**

## The fine print you never read

Every points program, gift card, and ticket in your pocket is a *closed-loop* promise from a single company. Read the terms and conditions and you'll find the get-out clauses hiding in plain sight:

- Points **expire** — usually on the company's schedule, not yours.
- The company can **devalue** them whenever it likes ("dynamic pricing," they call it with a smile).
- The program can be **changed or killed** with a press release.
- Your points are **not transferable** — you can't gift them, sell them, or trade them for something better.
- And if the business goes under? Your "balance" evaporates with it.

In economic terms, these are *closed-loop monetary systems* — you can only spend the points inside the shop that issued them, on the company's terms, at the company's pleasure. The point of the point system is to *trap you*, not to enrich you.

## What if your points were actually yours?

Here's the mental flip that changes everything: **what if every point, ticket, and membership lived on a blockchain — in a wallet *you* control?**

That's exactly what [CashTokens](https://cashtokens.org/) make possible on [Bitcoin Cash](https://bitcoincash.org/). Activated in the May 2023 network upgrade, CashTokens are *native* tokens that live directly on the Bitcoin Cash base layer — the same layer that secures every BCH coin. No sidechain, no bridging, no second-class citizenship.

That means they inherit everything that makes BCH valuable:

- **They're counterfeit-proof.** The network itself validates each token's origin. A wallet can instantly verify a token is genuine — no central database to trust.
- **They're yours.** Tokens live in your wallet, under your keys. No company can claw them back, expire them, or quietly devalue them.
- **They're cheap.** Moving a token costs fractions of a cent, settled in seconds. Yes, seconds — not days.
- **They're transferable.** Send them, sell them, trade them. On a peer-to-peer network, not a company's ledger.

## The two flavors: fungible and non-fungible

CashTokens come in two clean primitives, and the distinction matters for real life:

**Fungible tokens** are interchangeable — every unit is identical to every other, like a dollar bill. These are perfect for things where quantity matters:

- **Loyalty points** — your coffee shop issues 1,000 "RoastCoin" tokens. Every one is worth exactly one free espresso. You can trade them, save them, or sell them.
- **Gift cards** — a store card becomes a token balance you hold and can even resell.
- **Stocks & shares** — fractional ownership represented by fungible units.
- **Vouchers, coupons, prepaid credit** — anything denominated in a count.

**Non-fungible tokens (NFTs)** are unique and indivisible — each one is one-of-a-kind. These shine when uniqueness matters:

- **Event tickets** — a single NFT is your one seat at the show. And here's the killer feature: because it's a smart contract, a venue can issue tickets that **automatically send the organizer a cut every time the ticket is resold.** No scalpers hoarding, no counterfeit wristbands — a verified, royalty-paying ticket.
- **Memberships** — a "zero-length commitment" NFT that simply proves you're a member, verifiable by any contract.
- **Collectibles & digital art** — the fun side, but with real utility baked in (more on that below).
- **Identity & access** — a token that proves you belong, without revealing who you are.

## The self-custody superpower

Here's the part that should feel genuinely liberating. With a closed-loop points system, the company is the custodian. They hold your "balance" and you're just borrowing their promise.

With CashTokens, **you are the custodian.** The tokens sit under your own private keys. That's self-custody, and it changes the power dynamic completely:

- The issuer can't freeze, expire, or devalue your tokens after they're issued.
- You can move them, gift them, or sell them however you like.
- If the issuing company vanishes, your tokens still exist on the chain. Their worth might change, but *nobody can take them from you*.

It's the difference between renting an apartment and owning the deed.

## Real projects already doing it

This isn't vaporware — builders are shipping:

- **[BCH Guru](https://bch.guru/)** — a 10,000-NFT collection that's more than art. Holders get access to an on-chain price-prediction game where you stake BCH on predictions, plus an exclusive airdrop. Utility token, not just a jpeg.
- **[TapSwap](https://tapswap.cash/)** — the first non-custodial CashTokens marketplace, trading both fungible and non-fungible tokens directly on BCH. Sort by rarity, see live volumes, consolidate your UTXOs — all in your own wallet.
- **[Cauldron DEX](https://app.cauldron.quest/)** — a decentralized exchange on Bitcoin Cash using CashTokens. Instant swaps, near-zero fees. Its first trading pair (SOCKS/BCH) famously popped ~50x within hours of launch — a reminder that the demand for tradeable native tokens is real.
- **[CashToken Reward](https://cashtokenreward.com/)** — tooling for businesses to build token-based loyalty programs for customer reward and engagement.

Wallets like [Electron Cash](https://electroncash.org/), [Paytaca](https://www.paytaca.com/), [Cashonize](https://cashonize.com/), and [Zapit](https://www.zapit.io/) already handle CashTokens, so the ecosystem for actually *using* these is in place.

## Your loyalty, on your terms

So the next time you're handed a loyalty card, or a "non-transferable" ticket, or a gift card that expires, ask yourself one question:

**Who owns this?**

If the answer is "the company," you've just signed up to be a captive. The whole point of a points program is that *you* spend, *they* keep the ledger, and *you* get a shiny number that evaporates at their discretion.

CashTokens flip that script. They let a business issue the same reward — but hand you a token that is *yours*. Verifiable, transferable, impossible to confiscate, and dirt cheap to move. It's loyalty without the lock-in: you stay because the coffee is good and the reward is real, not because your points will vanish if you leave.

That's not a better loyalty program. That's a fundamentally fairer one. And it starts the moment your points live in *your* wallet, not *their* database.
