# What Is Bitcoin Cash?

Bitcoin Cash (BCH) is a decentralized, peer-to-peer electronic cash system. It
descends directly from the original Bitcoin project and keeps the core promise
of *"electronic cash"* — money you can send to anyone in the world, instantly
and at near-zero cost, without needing a bank or a trusted third party.

## The big idea

Bitcoin Cash was created to scale. By raising the block size limit, it lets more
transactions fit into every block, which keeps fees low even during periods of
high usage. The result is money that works for **everyday purchases**, not just
large transfers.

## Key properties

- **Permissionless** — anyone can send or receive BCH.
- **Peer-to-peer** — transactions go directly between users.
- **Low fees** — often fractions of a cent.
- **Transparent** — every transaction is recorded on a public ledger.

## Why it matters

Money should be useful for ordinary people. Bitcoin Cash focuses on being a
reliable medium of exchange — fast, cheap, and available to everyone.
