# Making Your First Payment

Once your wallet is set up, sending Bitcoin Cash is as easy as scanning a QR
code or pasting an address. Here's the general flow.

## Receiving BCH

1. Open your wallet and tap **Receive**.
2. Your wallet shows an address (and a QR code) starting with `bitcoincash:` or
   `q...`.
3. Share that address with the sender.

## Sending BCH

1. Tap **Send**.
2. Paste the recipient's address or scan their QR code.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Review the (tiny) network fee and confirm.

## A few tips

- **Double-check the address** — a single wrong character can send funds to the
  wrong place.
- **Start small** — send a tiny test amount first.
- **Fees are tiny** — usually well under a cent, so it's fine to send small
  amounts.

That's it! Once you've sent and received a couple of transactions, the basics
are behind you.
